# Literature status

Two preliminary searches and two final solution-aware searches are preserved
in this directory. They compare the proposed theorem with one-variable
compression rigidity, scalar Korovkin results, planar extreme-boundary
hyperrigidity, monomial hyperrigidity, and spectrahedral hyperrigidity.

The two separately run final searches agree on the following division:

- affine dimension at most one is already covered by Brown's one-variable
  theorem, and also by Scherer's theorem after the graph-hull lift;
- special planar functions such as positive-definite quadratics and certain
  radial powers are covered by Shankar and Pietrzycki--Stochel;
- no theorem was located through 2026-07-24 covering arbitrary planar compact
  convex \(K\), arbitrary continuous strictly convex \(g\), and all
  representations including diffuse spectra.

This supports the label **apparently new in affine dimension two**, with
moderate-to-high confidence. It does not prove absolute historical priority.
