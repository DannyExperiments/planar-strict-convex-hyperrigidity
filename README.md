## Main theorem

Let \(K\) be a compact convex set of affine dimension at most two, let \(E\)
be a normalized positive-operator-valued measure on \(K\), let \(F\) be a
projection-valued measure on \(K\), and let \(g:K\to\mathbb R\) be continuous
and strictly convex.  If

\[
\int_K\ell\,dE=\int_K\ell\,dF
\quad\text{for every affine }\ell,
\qquad
\int_Kg\,dE=\int_Kg\,dF,
\]

then

\[
E=F.
\]

Equivalently, the function system generated by the affine functions and
\(g\) has the unique-extension property for every representation of
\(C(K)\).

The affine-dimension-at-most-one part is previously known. The literature
candidate is the genuinely two-dimensional statement for arbitrary
continuous strictly convex \(g\), including diffuse representations.

## Original operator consequence

For commuting positive contractions \(X,Y\), a projection \(P\), commuting
compressions \(A=PXP|_{\operatorname{ran}P}\) and
\(B=PYP|_{\operatorname{ran}P}\), and continuous strictly convex
\(f:[0,1]^2\to\mathbb R\), the identity

\[
P f(X,Y)P|_{\operatorname{ran}P}=f(A,B)
\]

forces

\[
PX=XP,\qquad PY=YP.
\]
